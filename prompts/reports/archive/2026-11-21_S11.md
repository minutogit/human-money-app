## 3. Open Uncertainties / Design Questions (`S11-Q1`–`S11-Q3` All Resolved)

1. **`S11-Q1` (RESOLVED) — 24h Negative Cache on Transient Network Errors (`session.rs`, `state.rs`, `market_service.rs`):**
   - Switched `ActiveProfileCaches.media_negative_cache` to `expires_at_secs` with `>= 1024` expired-entry retention sweep before clear fallback; `get_ad_image_impl` applies `86_400s` (24h) on permanent misses (`Ok(None)` 404 or failed `open_media_blob` decryption) vs `60s` on transient transport/HTTP errors (`Err(_)`).
2. **`S11-Q2` (RESOLVED) — `get_market_view_impl` & `get_ad_image_impl` Under `session_timeout_seconds == 0` (`session.rs`, `state.rs`, `market_service.rs`, `transfer_service.rs`, `sync_engine/{mod,channel_reader}.rs`):**
   - Pre-cached `card_store` (`MARKET_CARD_STORE_KEY`) and `sync_state` (`MARKET_SYNC_KEY`) in `ActiveProfileCaches` during `initialize_profile_session_fallible`, introduced `load_card_store_or_cached` / `load_sync_state_or_cached` + cache-synced saves across all write paths (propagating disk corruption while gracefully falling back to session-verified cache on `SessionNotActive` when `password.is_none()`), and guarded encrypted local sidecar reads/writes in `get_ad_image_impl` so Marketplace board views & remote ad images work cleanly under `session_timeout_seconds == 0`.
3. **`S11-Q3` (RESOLVED) — Shortening Non-Zero `session_timeout_seconds` on Active Sessions (`settings_service.rs`):**
   - `patch_security_policy_impl` captures `prev_timeout` and, when `updated.session_timeout_seconds > 0 && updated.session_timeout_seconds < prev_timeout && service.is_session_active()`, immediately re-unlocks under the shorter duration if `password` was supplied or locks the active session + clears one-shot PIN grant otherwise (gating coordinator wake on `service.is_session_active()`).
